Whether you dry clothes indoors or on a balcony, a good rack and basket make laundry day easier. These picks are collected from Shopee Malaysia.

  • Check the folded size, so the rack stores flat when you are not using it.
  • Look for the weight limit or number of rails in the listing before you pick one.
  • Wheels and foldable baskets help in small flats, but check the listed dimensions.
  • Compare the open size of the rack with your balcony or room space.
